## Editorial guardrails

The narrative should acknowledge achievement and hardship together. Migration, loss, constitutional change, political instability, conflict, and economic challenges should be described with restraint and without graphic detail or partisan language. Quotes should be short, attributed, and treated as historical captions rather than invented slogans. The “Pakistan Today” chapter should frame 2026 as a moment of possibility, using the population and youth facts above without implying that demographics alone guarantee prosperity.
